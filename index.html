OUR STORY

How ANERRA bloomed.

ANERRA began with a love for beautiful things, thoughtful gifting and the joy of creating something that feels truly your own.

We believe there's something special about choosing the little details — the flowers, the colours and the finishing touches — and making something with meaning behind it.

ANERRA brings together floral gifting and creative experiences, giving you different ways to celebrate, create and make something beautiful.

From thoughtfully arranged bouquets made to be gifted, to our Bloom Bar where you can create your own, ANERRA is all about making flowers feel a little more personal.

And because we love things that last, our handmade pipe-cleaner blooms bring a playful, everlasting twist to traditional florals.

But flowers are only the beginning.

ANERRA is growing into a collection of experiences inspired by flowers, adornment, creativity and culture — including our Jhumka Bar and our upcoming Gajra Bar.

Whatever you choose, we want you to leave with something that feels a little bit special.

OUR MISSION

To make beautiful things feel personal.

At ANERRA, our mission is simple: to make beautiful things feel personal.

We want to create experiences that bring together creativity, flowers, culture and the joy of making something with your own hands.

Whether you're gifting a bouquet, creating one at our Bloom Bar, designing your own jhumkas or wearing a fresh gajra, we want every ANERRA experience to feel thoughtful, playful and uniquely yours.
